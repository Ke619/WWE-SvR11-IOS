A fix release, mostly from your **Report a problem** reports - thank you for sending them.

## 🛠️ Fixes
- **Creating an online account no longer asks for an invite code** when the server doesn't need one (the box only shows when it does).
- **The game closed after winning** a match, during the replay highlights - fixed.
- **Extreme Rules: a wrestler got stuck searching under the ring forever** when more weapons were picked than there are places - the extra weapons now wait their turn.
- **CPUs standing still**: a CPU stuck outside the ring while the referee counts climbs back in, and two CPUs stuck in a lock-up go on with it.
- **An arena mod built on SmackDown loaded the stock arena** when it sat on another arena's tile - it plays the mod now.
- **Superstar Threads lost the DLC superstars** once a mod was listed there - fixed.
- **Mods with accents in their name** (e.g. Finn Bálor) played no entrance movie - fixed.
- **Online**: two players whose weapon mods were installed in a different order no longer get different weapons under the ring.
- **Mod Maker**: SvR 2007 / 2008 divas (Lita, Trish, Melina, Mickie) had bent, twisted legs - fixed (convert them again).

## ✨ Changed
- **Online mods**: a session can now fetch up to **4 GB** of the host's mods (was 1 GB).
- **Report a problem** groups reports better (the game's harmless start-up messages no longer get mistaken for the problem).

Everything new in 2.1.6 is in the [2.1.6 notes](https://github.com/KaikoClanworth1/wwe-svr2011-pc/releases/tag/v2.1.6).
