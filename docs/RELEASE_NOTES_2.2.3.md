**Download:** `SvR2011-PC-v2.2.3.zip` for PC (Windows 8.1 or newer, Linux / Steam Deck through Proton) - or `SvR2011-Android-v2.2.3.apk` on its own for Android. `SvR2011-Mods-v2.2.3.zip` holds the bundled mods - the Android app downloads it by itself. `SvR2011-Switch-v2.2.3.nsp` for a modded Nintendo Switch (see `docs/SWITCH_INSTALL.md`). On 2.1.x or 2.2.x? The launcher and the Android app update themselves - **close the Mod Maker before updating**.

⚠️ **Only download the port from this GitHub page.** It's free. If someone sends you to Telegram or asks you to pay, it isn't us.

Fixes marked "potential" couldn't be fully proven here, so please report it if the problem is still there.

## 🆕 New
- **Tap the tag team and 6-man selects** on phones (and click them with the PC mouse): your slot, every wrestler's pick, and CONTROL PARTNERS YES / NO.
- **Check entrance numbers** in Mods (PC launcher and Android app): mods installed before 2.2.2 that share an entrance number with a different entrance are moved to a free number, so each plays its own. It also runs once by itself after updating, and tells you what moved.

## 🛠️ Fixes
- **Big square shadows under the ring ropes** - gone. The ropes now cast thin shadows (every version had this).
- **Android: slow motion after a pin**, then in the replays, menus and next match - fixed (a 2.2.2 rule for CPU kick-outs stayed on after the match).
- **Android: wrong textures in big modded arenas**, and the stutter then close after the match there - fixed / potential fix.
- **A crash loading some modded arenas** (Android) - potential fix.
- **The game stuck loading a match** (seen in a triple threat with lots of move packs and DLC Vol. 2): the game now finds memory for big move files instead of waiting forever - potential fix.
- **Big roster: some superstar mods missing** from Create a Move-Set's Downloaded Superstar list - fixed (the Community Creations move-set list too - potential).
- **Commentators not saying wrestlers' names** with entrances off - potential fix.
- **Report a problem showed the wrong match** after a Road to WrestleMania backstage segment - potential fix.
- **Online, behind a carrier's router** (no direct connection possible): the game now says the match can't connect and explains why, instead of a relay warning that didn't help.
- Phones no longer write the same mod lines to the log on every frame.
