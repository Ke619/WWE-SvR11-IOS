## 🆕 New
- **Your own CAW glasses**: make glasses from an OBJ and a PNG on the Mod Maker's new **CAW glasses** page (Media tab), then wear them in Create A Superstar. PC and Android can install them. Glasses only for now; they sit on the head and don't follow the face sliders, so they can look off on a heavily reshaped face. Without the mod, a CAW shows stock glasses.
- **Graphics options**: **CROWD DENSITY**, and a **PRESET** row (Mali, Snapdragon, Switch, Steam Deck, Low / Medium / High end PC).
- **CONTROLS** gets **KEYBOARD**, **MOUSE** and **CONTROLLER** tabs: one controller layout per player, used during play; on-screen prompts follow it.
- **Touch screens**: tap the menu lists, OPTIONS rows, the select grid and the VS screen.
- **Title matches in exhibition**: the game's own title prompt, and a win changes the real champion. Offline only.
- **Victory track** for superstar mods and media packs, and the song no longer restarts on the victory scene. Offline only for now: the victory track isn't played at the end of online matches.
- **Mod Maker For Fun page**: export a whole arena or superstar to FBX with textures and lights; a superstar export re-imports through the FBX import.
- **DLC packages from other games** in the porters: arenas, props, trons and moves (388 DLC-only moves listed - potential, only a few tested).
- **WWE 2K14 entrances** keep their own lights and effects (potential improvement).

## ⭐ Discovery
- **Star ratings and comments** on every mod's page: rate a mod 1-5 stars, read and write comments, and sort the list by **Top rated**. Needs a signed-in online account. Links and talk about copies of games aren't allowed; any comment can be reported.

## 📝 Reports
- **Reports and suggestions now need a signed-in online account and your Discord name** (we answer there; only we see it). The launcher, the app, the Mod Maker and the in-game REPORT A PROBLEM all ask for it and remember it. Older versions are told to update.

## 🌐 Online
- **DLC is compared by its name**: two players with the same DLC no longer refuse each other (potential fix). A missing DLC is still refused.
- **Match setup check**: at the start of an online match, the two games compare who is in it, the rule and the arena. A difference is shown on screen and logged so it can be tracked down; the match still goes on.
