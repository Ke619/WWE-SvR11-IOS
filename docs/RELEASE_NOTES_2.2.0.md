**Download:** `SvR2011-PC-v2.2.0.zip` for PC (Windows 8.1 or newer, Linux / Steam Deck through Proton) - or `SvR2011-Android-v2.2.0.apk` on its own for Android. `SvR2011-Mods-v2.2.0.zip` holds the bundled mods - the Android app downloads it by itself. On 2.1.x? The launcher and the Android app update themselves - **close the Mod Maker before updating**.

⚠️ **Only download the port from this GitHub page.** It's free. If someone sends you to Telegram or asks you to pay, it isn't us.

A big release: new mod types, better online connections, and many fixes from your **Report a problem** reports - thank you.

Fixes marked "potential" couldn't be fully proven here, so please report it if the problem is still there. **The Android changes in this release weren't tested on phones this round.**

## 📌 After updating
- **The first start takes longer once**: the game merges your move packs again. Let it finish.
- **Port moves again** in the Mod Maker to get the porter fixes below, then pick the moves again in CREATE A MOVE-SET.
- **Texture packs:** old pack files for very small textures (16 pixels or less on a side) are now ignored. Dump those textures again if you changed them.
- **Mod Maker:** add your game disc folders once under My game discs.

## 🆕 New
- **Title mods**: replace the game's titles - name, holder's name, art and belt textures. Mod Maker: Make > Media > Titles. Android can install them too.
- **DLC superstars' attires** can now be added and replaced (Mod Maker's Attires page).
- **DLC characters from other games**: the Mod Maker's porters can read your own DLC packages from another game, including DLC superstars' entrances and titantrons.
- **Suggestions**: a new tab in the launcher, and My suggestions on Android. Send an idea and see our answer: Sure, No / not planned, or Done in a version.
- **More superstar mods at once now works online** 🧪: the 317-mod option follows the usual online mod rules, and everyone needs the mod. It's still being worked on and can corrupt your save, so **back up your Saves folder first**.
- **Story Designer stories follow mods**: a mod moved to another number keeps its place in your story.
- **A created wrestler's Menu Screen Pose** now plays on the select screen.
- **Online Page check** (launcher and Android): shows your router type, IPv6 and firewall state, and what to do about them.
- **Mod Doctor**: a new button moves a mod's moves on numbers the game can't play.
- **Reports and suggestions can't contain links** any more.

## 🌐 Online
- **More players connect directly, without the relay's extra delay**: both games try every address they have, including IPv6, and the fastest one wins. The ping box names the path: direct, IPv6, LAN or relay.
- **The CPU tab's settings now apply in online matches** - before, every online match played AUTO. Potential fix: tell us if a setting doesn't reach the match.
- **The same mod no longer looks different on two PCs**, so you aren't told you lack a bundled mod you have. Really different copies get a clear message.
- **A different set of DLC** is refused with a message naming the package.

## 🛠️ Fixes
- **Pin count at 30 fps and on slower phones**: nobody could kick out, or the count ended early - potential fix.
- **Hell in a Cell with Tables & Ladders**: picking one up froze your wrestler - potential fix.
- **Stuck searching under the ring, or after picking up some weapons**: those weapons now use the steel chair's animations, so a dumbbell swings like a chair but keeps its own damage - potential fix.
- **Frozen wrestlers or crashes with many move packs** (lost strike animations) - potential fix.
- **Crashes - all potential fixes:** at the highlights after a match; right after the entrances; a modded superstar's crowd signs at match load; an empty mod slot's model; Road to WrestleMania, Jericho's week with Mark Henry's run-in.
- **Crowd signs from superstar mods** show in the crowd again.
- **Road to WrestleMania, VS. UNDERTAKER**: a mod ran through walls and froze on the backstage walk - potential fix.
- **Story Designer**: NEW no longer crashes once you have many mods.
- **Move-sets on DLC-slot mods** are kept after you edit them.
- **Moves with no animation** in older ports (Sin Cara and others): the superstar's base move is used instead of getting stuck in a corner - potential fix. Sabu's stuck Springboard DDT and Camel Clutch - potential fix (Sabu 1.9).
- **Entrance movies - potential fixes:** black mod trons on Android, a green picture on some phones, green lines at the bottom on PC, and smoother entrances on Mali phones.
- **Mouse and keyboard mode**: a freeze during movies and Alt+F4 hanging - potential fix.
- **Black rows on the ring mat** from texture packs - potential fix.
- **Mod Maker** (all potential fixes - port again):
  - ported WWE '12-2K17 moves no longer land on a DLC move or a move number the game never starts, and their camera follows the whole move;
  - long submissions like the Kimura Lock are kept;
  - SvR 2007-2009 signatures and finishers (Rated RKO, Dragonrana, Hunting RKO) use 2011's own versions where it has them;
  - FBX import no longer tips the head forward with the neck.
- **Android**:
  - a long first start after adding mods now says "Preparing your mods" and doesn't blame the graphics driver;
  - missing game files are named, and an incomplete install goes to Install with your saves and mods kept;
  - a package zip never replaces your existing saves or mods.
- The launcher also checks for missing must-have game files before offering Play.
