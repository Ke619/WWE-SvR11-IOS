## 🎮 Nintendo Switch (new)
- A Switch version for a modded console: put `SvR2011-Switch-v2.2.2.nsp` in a folder named `Switch` beside `SvR2011 Launcher.exe`, renamed to `svr2011.nsp`. Then use the launcher's **Setup -> Switch** to make the files on your SD card, and install `svr2011.nsp` with DBI. The full steps are in `docs/SWITCH_INSTALL.md`. You need your own game files; we can't help with keys or firmware.
- Report problems from the game: **REPORT A PROBLEM** (in OPTIONS and the pause menu). Sign in on the PC launcher's Online tab first; Setup -> Switch carries the sign-in over.

## 🆕 New
- **Mouse in the menus**: point and click the menu lists, OPTIONS rows, the select grid and the VS screen. In a match with the mouse-and-keyboard mode on, the mouse stays the camera.
- **Ported arenas keep their own crowd**: arenas from SvR 2007, 2008, 2009 and Legends of WrestleMania bring their own game's crowd, so people sit in that arena's stands instead of floating or sinking (One Night Stand). The Mod Maker asks: the source's own crowd, the host's, or none.
- **Crowd lighting** in the Mod Maker's Lighting panel: keep an arena's crowd colour, make it neutral, pick a colour, or change its brightness (no more red or blue crowd on ported arenas unless you want it).
- **Tag team moves** in the porters: a ported double-team move now plays the partner's own part (potential - tested with forced moves, Doomsday Device, Uso Splash and others). The Mod Maker names the slots Standing / Corner double team. Tag-team finisher-style moves are kept back for now.
- **Tag and trios entrances** in the entrance porter: choose Singles, Tag team or Trios. Team entrances go in CREATE AN ENTRANCE's tag and trio lists and play in its preview with the team's members (potential - not yet seen in a real tag match). A team entrance can also be used as a singles one.
- **WWE 2K17 DLC** superstars list in the porter (potential).

## ⭐ Discovery
- **Upvotes instead of stars**: one upvote per player per mod, and a **Most upvoted** sort. Older versions can't rate any more - update.
- **Authors can turn comments off** on their own mods.
- **Installed tick and Update button**: a tick shows mods you have, and an **↑ UPDATE** button shows when yours is older - update straight from the grid or from Installed Mods, keeping the mod switched on or off as it was.
- **Roomier layout**: bigger tiles that fit the screen at any scale, and comments shown in full.

## 👤 Accounts
- **Clear password rules**: at least 8 characters, not your account name, not a common password, and a mix of letters, numbers or symbols. The launcher and the app show the rule and check it as you type. A refused password no longer uses up a reset code.
- Suggestions are for the game and its tools - not requests for mods.

## 🛠️ Fixes
- **CPU never kicking out of pins** below 60 fps (phones, 30 fps): fixed - while a CPU is pinned the count runs a little slower there, so it can kick out.
- **Freeze after a match** (the known hang while the next match loads) - potential fix.
- **Android: slowdown from the second match on** (memory filling up on some phones) - potential fix.
- **Mod Maker**: a WWE 2K17 DLC prop file no longer stops an entrance port (Goldberg); WWE 2K16 DLC arenas port again instead of being taken for backstage rooms.
- **Online**: new checks log whether the two games in a match stay in step, and show "THE TWO GAMES ARE OUT OF STEP" if they don't - so the "playing against a bot" reports can be tracked down.
